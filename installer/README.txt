Bible Languages Studio 1.0.0 - by Larry Dalton

Read, write and study Biblical Hebrew (with vowels and cantillation), Paleo-Hebrew and Koine Greek, on your own computer.
- Hebrew Old Testament (Westminster Leningrad Codex), Greek New Testament (SBLGNT) and the Septuagint (Swete)
- Click any word: transliteration, grammar, dictionary (Brown-Driver-Briggs, Abbott-Smith), every occurrence
- Interlinear view, Septuagint beside the Hebrew, concordance across all three texts
- Write in Hebrew, Paleo-Hebrew or Greek; English names by sound; English to Hebrew or Greek drafts
- Export SVG, PNG and JPEG and read them back

Install: extract this folder, then double-click Install.cmd.
The studio installs for your Windows account only (no administrator rights), runs at http://localhost:8769/
and starts automatically when you sign in. The infographic is copied to your Desktop.

Uninstall: run Uninstall.ps1 from %LOCALAPPDATA%\BibleLanguagesStudio, or use Settings > Apps if you used the setup program.
