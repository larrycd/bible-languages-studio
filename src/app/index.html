About

Bible Languages Studio reads, writes and studies the languages of the Bible: Biblical Hebrew with vowel points (nikkud) and cantillation, the old Paleo-Hebrew script, and Koine Greek with breathings and accents. It was designed and built by Larry Dalton.

  • Bible: the whole Hebrew Old Testament (Westminster Leningrad Codex), the Septuagint (Swete's edition) and the Greek New Testament (SBL Greek New Testament), with every word's dictionary form, grammar and meaning; interlinear view; Septuagint alongside the Hebrew.
  • Write: type Hebrew or Greek, a transliteration, Beta Code, or English words and names; export SVG or PNG and check that it reads back.
  • Translate: rule-based English drafts in Biblical Hebrew (verb first, construct chains, object marker, prefixes) and Koine Greek (cases, articles, verb endings), using forms found in the Bible.
  • Dictionary and Concordance: Brown-Driver-Briggs for Hebrew and Aramaic, Abbott-Smith for Greek, with every occurrence in the OT, Septuagint and NT.
