Data created by www.STEPBible.org based on work at Tyndale House Cambridge (CC BY 4.0).
Source: https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data

Used in Bible Languages Studio:
- TAHOT (Translators Amalgamated Hebrew OT): Hebrew words, transliterations, English glosses,
  extended Strong's numbers and morphology codes. The Hebrew text is the Westminster Leningrad
  Codex (public domain) with Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible morphology (CC BY 4.0).
- TAGNT (Translators Amalgamated Greek NT): the words of the SBL Greek New Testament (CC BY 4.0,
  Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software), with glosses, Strong's numbers and morphology.
- TBESG (Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Greek, based on Abbott-Smith).
- TBESH (Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Hebrew): only the lemma, transliteration,
  part of speech and the Tyndale English gloss. The definitions column (Abridged BDB by Online Bible)
  is NOT used.

Changes made: the data was converted into compact JSON files (one per book), indexes were built for
search, concordance and drafting, Greek NT words were filtered to the SBLGNT edition, and HTML markup
in the Greek lexicon was simplified. No words, glosses or tags were edited.
