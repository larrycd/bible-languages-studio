---
Bible Languages Studio uses the Septuagint text and Morpheus analysis (data/morph-morpheus) from the
QuantForge LXX project (https://github.com/QuantForgeSoftware/lxx), based on H. B. Swete, The Old
Testament in Greek according to the Septuagint (1909-1930, public domain).
Changes made: converted to compact JSON; Latin look-alike letters inside Greek words replaced with Greek
letters; OCR verse numbers, superscripts, section marks and line-break hyphens removed; elision marks
unified; untagged capitalised words given the analysis of the same word elsewhere in the text; lemmas
linked to Strong's numbers. The derived files data/text/lxx_*.json and data/conc/lxx.json are shared
under CC BY-SA 4.0.
